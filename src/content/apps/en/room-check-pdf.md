Coming soon to Google Play.

## Who it's for

- **Tenants**: Keep photo evidence of existing scratches and stains before moving in, so you're prepared when it's time to get your deposit back.
- **Landlords and cleaning companies**: Inspect and record the condition of multiple properties in a consistent format.

When you first open the app, you choose between Tenant mode and Property management mode.

## Features

- **Room-by-room records**: For each room and each area (walls, floor, ceiling, windows, doors), record photos, the condition (scratch, stain, damage, mold), and comments. Everything is saved automatically.
- **Built-in camera**: Take up to 10 photos in a row for each area. You can choose whether to record the shooting location in the photos.
- **PDF reports**: Compile your records into an A4 PDF, preview it, and share it.
- **Property management** (Property management mode): Manage inspections by property and unit, and view upcoming and past inspections. You can also fill property names and inspection dates into your own PDF templates.
- **Japanese and English**: Use the app and create PDFs in Japanese or English.

## Your data

Photos and everything you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. Recording inspections and creating PDFs work offline.

## Pricing

Free to use, with ads. Property management mode offers a monthly plan that removes ads and adds more features.

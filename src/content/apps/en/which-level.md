## Who it's for

- Anyone who has wondered "which level did I park on?" at a mall or airport parking garage
- Drivers who want to find their car underground, where GPS is weak, using the level, pillar number and a photo

## Features

- **Just open it**: The moment you open the app, the time and location are saved automatically.
- **Snap the pillar sign**: Take a photo of the pillar or level sign. The app spots text like "C-12" or "3F" and lets you add it with one tap. You can leave a memo, too.
- **Arrow and map**: A big arrow points to your car and shows how far away it is. Switch to the map to see the surroundings.
- **Which floor you're on**: Using your phone's barometer, it gives you a rough idea of where you are, like "Car on 3F, you are on about 1F".
- **Parking fee estimate** (Japan only): Take a photo of the fee sign. The text is read on your device, and the fee so far is calculated from how long you've been parked.
- **Saved when you leave your car** (Android only): When your phone disconnects from your car's Bluetooth, your spot is saved. Android Auto is supported.
- **History and favorites**: Star the places you visit often, and search by place, memo, level or pillar.

Floor estimates and fee estimates are approximate. Please also check the signs around you.

## Your data

Your records and photos are stored on your device and are currently not sent to our servers. No account needed. Even without a signal underground, your photos and level notes are still there.

In the future, we may add features that use cloud AI to read signs more accurately. If we do, we'll describe what is sent in the privacy policy and let you choose whether to use them.

## Pricing

Free to use with ads. The ad-free version (one-time purchase) removes ads, shows your full history, lets you add more than 3 favorites, and lets you keep photos longer than 7 days, up to forever.
